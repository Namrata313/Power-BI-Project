# Power-BI-Project
## Project Obectives
To build an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard to analyze sales, revenue, quantity, and customer behaviour across product categories, regions, sales channels, and customer types, and to help business take data-driven decisions to increase revenue.


Dataset :-<a href ="https://github.com/Namrata313/Power-BI-Project/blob/main/market%20data.xlsx">

## KPI Tracked
Total Sales: 1.20M
Total Orders: 1,174
Total Quantity Sold: 7K
Total Revenue: 3.05M

## Process 
* Verify data for any missing values and anomalies, and sort out the same.
* Made sure data is consistent and clean with respect to data type, data format.
* Created DAX measures and visuals according to the questions asked.
* Merge all visuals into one dashboard and apply slicer to make dynamic.

## 10 Business Questions This Dashboard Answers
-What is total sales, revenue, orders and quantity?
-Which product category generates highest revenue?
-Which product generates highest revenue?
-Which region is performing best in revenue?
-Which sales channel brings most revenue - Online, Corporate, Retail Store or Marketplace?
-What is the share of Returning vs New customers?
-Which category is underperforming and needs attention?
-Which region needs more marketing focus?
-How does revenue vary by product within a category?Which customer type should we retain to grow  revenue?

 ## Project Insights
-Computers dominate: Computers = 1.88M revenue, which is 62% of total revenue. Monitors second at 0.60M.
-Low performers: Accessories (0.07M) and Office Supplies (0.02M) contribute very low revenue.
-Top Product: Wi-Fi Router is top product with 49K revenue, followed by Webcam 20K, USB Drive 128GB 15K.
-Top Regions: Central (0.70M) and North (0.70M) are top regions. East 0.58M, South 0.55M, West 0.55M are almost equal.
-Customer Loyalty is key: Returning customers contribute 69% revenue, New customers only 31%. Retention is more profitable than         acquisition.
-Channel Mix: Online 31.00% and Corporate Sales 30.03% are top channels. Marketplace is lowest at 13.22%.
-Average Order Value: 3.05M / 1,174 = ∼2.6K per order.

## Final Conclusion
-Focus more on Computers and Monitors
-Improve sales of Accessories and Office Supplies with offers/bundling
-Invest in retention programs for Returning customers
-Improve Marketplace channel and focus on East, South, West regions to balance growth.
